From Istanbul to Quincy

Our Story

At Spincy, we believe food is more than fuel — it’s comfort, culture, and connection.

Our love for chicken döner was born in the vibrant streets of Istanbul, where the aroma of fire-roasted meat, the rhythm of busy sidewalks, and the joy of sharing meals with loved ones fills every corner.

That tradition inspired us to bring the same flavor, passion, and hospitality to Quincy, Illinois — a place we now proudly call home.

Every Spincy wrap is handcrafted with marinated chicken, warm lavash, fresh vegetables, and bold, savory sauce — all served with care and love.

Whether you’re grabbing a quick bite or connecting with friends, we’re here to turn every meal into a flavorful moment that brings people together.

Wrap It Forward

Our Mission

At Spincy, our mission goes beyond serving great food — we want to nourish our community in every sense.

Inspired by the Turkish tradition of askıda ekmek, we’ve launched the Wrap It Forward program: a simple way for customers to donate a meal for someone in need.

Whether you donate $1 or cover an entire wrap, your kindness goes directly to feeding someone who could use a warm, delicious meal.

We’re also proud to support families using SNAP (EBT/Food Stamps). If you show a valid SNAP card, you’ll receive 50% off your first meal — because everyone deserves access to warm, nourishing food, no matter their situation.

This is a Spincy-funded discount; it does not mean SNAP benefits can be used to purchase hot prepared meals.

At Spincy, we believe food is a human right — and when we share it, we bring hope, dignity, and connection.
